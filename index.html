Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is Swapaya out yet?
Not quite — we're building it right now. We're opening across London first, then beyond. Join the waitlist and we'll invite you the moment it's live in your area.
Is it really free?
Yes. No fees, no subscriptions, no prices. You swap one of your things for one of theirs (or a small bundle). Nobody takes a cut.
Why are you building this?
We throw out mountains of good things, then go and buy them all again. Swapaya is our answer: a human way to keep good stuff moving — swapped locally, handed over with trust. It's not another place to buy and sell — it's a place to swap, and give good things a second life.
How do swaps work — in person or by post?
Either. Meet someone nearby and swap face to face, or post it if that's easier. You decide what works for each swap.
Who pays for postage?
You and the other swapper agree that when you arrange the swap — most people just each post their own item to the other. There's no Swapaya fee on top; postage is the only cost, and it's yours to sort between you. If you'd rather not post at all, meet up in person instead.
How do swaps stay safe and fair?
You see photos and chat before you commit to anything, so nothing's a surprise. Meeting in person? The app suggests safe, public places roughly halfway between you, so you're not heading somewhere unfamiliar on your own. Posting instead? Agree the details up front and use tracked delivery. After every swap you rate each other, so the community stays good to swap with — and people who don't play fair don't last.
How can I help shape it?
Tell us what you'd swap first when you join the waitlist — it genuinely shapes what we build and how we launch. You can also follow along on Instagram, TikTok and LinkedIn to see how it's going.
How do I use Swapaya?
Swapaya is a mobile app. We're launching on Android first, with iPhone coming soon. Join the waitlist and we'll let you know when it's available for your device.
